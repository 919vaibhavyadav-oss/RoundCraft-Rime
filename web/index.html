Panel interview practice

You can rehearse questions.
You can't rehearse a panel.

Three interviewers share one room. They hand off to each other, follow up on the number you just said, and keep talking when you talk over them. Each one speaks in their own Rime voice, switched per turn on a single connection.

The hard part isn't stopping the audio. When you cut across one interviewer, a different one takes over — so the half-finished sentence, and any lookup still running, must be fenced off. Otherwise you hear one interviewer's words in another's voice, and get scored on a sentence you never heard.

Uses your microphone. Interrupt whenever you like — that's the point.
