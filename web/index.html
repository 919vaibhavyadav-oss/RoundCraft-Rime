Panel interview practice

You can rehearse questions.
You can't rehearse being interrupted.

Three interviewers share one room. They hand off to each other, follow up on the number you just said, and keep going when you talk over them — each in their own Rime voice, switched per turn on a single connection.

The hard part isn't stopping the audio. When you cut across one interviewer, a different one takes over. The half-finished sentence and any lookup still running have to be fenced off — or you hear one interviewer's words in another's voice, and get assessed on a sentence you never heard.

Held for this session only. Never saved, never logged. Email addresses, phone numbers and links are stripped before the panel sees it.
